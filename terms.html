2. Who can use Skeeto

  • You must be at least 13 to create a Skeeto account. If you live in Quebec and are under 14, you need a parent's or guardian's consent. If you're under the age of majority where you live, a parent or guardian should read these terms with you.
  • One account per person. Don't use another person's account, and don't pretend to be someone else.
  • Keep access to your account to yourself. You're responsible for what happens on it.
  • If you've been banned from Skeeto, don't create another account.

3. The Feed rules

These are the rules you accept in the app before you post or message, exactly as the app shows them.

Before you post or message

Skeeto is for students, and it has no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users.

Not allowed

  • Nudity or sexual content of any kind.
  • Harassment, bullying, threats, hate, or content that encourages violence or self-harm.
  • Sharing someone's private information or photos without their permission, or pretending to be someone else.
  • Spam, scams, and anything illegal.
  • These rules cover everything you put on Skeeto: posts, comments, images, your profile, communities, messages and group chats.

What happens

  • Anything that breaks these rules is removed, and so are accounts that abuse other people.
  • Every image is checked automatically. One the first check is unsure about stays visible only to you until a second check decides.
  • An image confirmed as explicit is a strike: it is removed and the Feed is closed to you for 7 days.
  • Third strike and you can never post or comment again.

Reporting and blocking

  • Report any post, comment, profile or community, or someone in a chat, and it goes straight to Skeeto. Enough reports hide a post, comment or community until it is reviewed.
  • Block someone and they disappear for you everywhere, messages included, and you for them.

Your courses and program

  • Your courses, program and year from ACORN tune your Feed and put you in your course communities, which you can leave. Your program and year appear on your profile.

Who can join

  • You must be at least 13. You are responsible for what you post and send.
  • Questions, appeals, or something we missed: lucasnadon3132@gmail.com.

The rules apply to everything you put on Skeeto: posts, comments, images, links, stickers, messages and group chats (including a group's name), your profile (username, display name, bio, social handles and photo), and the communities you create.

4. How the rules are enforced

  • Automatic checks. Words that are never allowed are refused when you post. Text and images are checked automatically, and content that clearly breaks the rules is removed. A profile whose text breaks the rules is reset to a neutral username, with its bio, program and handles cleared. The privacy policy explains how the checks work.
  • Strikes. An image confirmed as explicit by the second image check is a strike. A strike removes the image and closes the Feed to you for 7 days. That includes the Feed itself, communities, posts and search. With a third strike, you can never post or comment again.
  • Removal and bans. Anything that breaks the rules is removed, whether it's found automatically, through reports, or by the developer. Accounts that abuse other people are banned from posting, and they may be removed from Skeeto entirely.
  • Strikes and bans follow the person, not the account. If you delete your account and sign up again with the same email address, or the same Apple or Google account, your strikes and any ban come back.
  • Community creators can remove posts, comments and members in the communities they create. They must follow these rules too.
  • Appeals. If you think a removal or strike was wrong, including one made automatically, email lucasnadon3132@gmail.com. A person will look at it. Strikes made in error are reversed, and wrongly removed content is restored.

5. Reporting and blocking

  • Report any post, comment, profile or community from its menu. In a chat, report the person from the top of the conversation. The person you report isn't told who reported them.
  • Every report is sent to the developer. If three different people report the same post, comment or student-created community, it's hidden at once until it's reviewed. Skeeto aims to review reports within 24 hours. Objectionable content is removed, and so are accounts that abuse other people.
  • Block someone, and they disappear for you everywhere, and you for them: the Feed, comments, reposts, search, profiles and messages. Blocking also ends any connection or follow between you.
  • If someone is in danger, don't wait for a report. Call 911.
