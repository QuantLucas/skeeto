Skeeto, Support

Last updated 14 October 2026

Skeeto is a campus app for U of T students at Mississauga, St. George and Scarborough. It brings together your timetable from ACORN, your assignments from Quercus, the campus map, walking times and food on campus, plus a Feed, Friends and messages for students.

Skeeto is an independent app operated by Lucas Nadon. It isn't affiliated with the University of Toronto.

Contact

Email lucasnadon3132@gmail.com. It helps to include your Skeeto username, your iPhone model and iOS version, and what you were doing when the problem happened. The same address handles appeals, privacy requests, and reports of anything that shouldn't be on Skeeto.

If someone is in danger, call 911. Don't wait for an email reply.

Delete your account

  1. Open Skeeto and go to the Account tab.
  2. Under Skeeto, tap Delete account and confirm.

This permanently deletes your Skeeto account and what it stored on Skeeto's server. The privacy policy lists exactly what's deleted, and the few things kept for moderation and safety. Your timetable, assignments and reminders stay on your phone. They work without an account.

If the app can't delete your account, email lucasnadon3132@gmail.com from the address your account uses, or include your username, and it will be deleted by hand.

Common questions

  • Does Skeeto see my UTORid password? No. You sign in on U of T's own page, and the app never reads or stores your password.
  • My timetable or assignments stopped updating. Go to Account and tap Reconnect ACORN under U of T if it is there. U of T ends its sessions from time to time; your timetable and due dates stay on your phone meanwhile.
  • Remove Skeeto's access to Quercus. In Quercus, go to Account → Settings → Approved Integrations and delete "Skeeto app" (or "Oakhall app").
  • Turn off usage analytics. Account → Privacy → Help improve Skeeto.
  • Report or block someone. Use the menu on any post, comment, profile or community, the top of a chat, or press and hold any message. Blocked people disappear for you everywhere, and you for them.
  • I got a strike I think is wrong. Email us. A person will review it, and a strike given in error is reversed.
  • Why do my courses, program and year show up? If you have an account, they personalise your Feed, put you in your course communities (which you can leave), and appear on your profile. The privacy policy explains this.
