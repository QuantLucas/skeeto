A friend wants to add you

Skeeto shows when you and your friends are both free between classes, and lets you message them.

Get Skeeto on the App Store

This button also copies your invite. After installing, open Skeeto and tap Paste on the “Add your friends” screen, or in Friends.

Already have Skeeto? Open it

Nothing happens, or Skeeto says the page isn’t there? Update Skeeto from the App Store and open this link again. Or find them in Friends › Add by the @handle in their message.

  • Your timetable comes from ACORN in one sign-in, nothing to type.
  • New friends see busy times only: when you're free, with no course names or rooms.
  • You choose what each person sees, and can stop sharing any time.

Opening this link adds no one by itself. Skeeto shows you who sent it, and you become friends only when you tap Add. Friends are kept to one campus, so it works only if you're both at the same one.
