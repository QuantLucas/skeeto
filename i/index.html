A friend wants to add you

Skeeto shows when you and your friends are both free between classes, and lets you message them.

Open in Skeeto Get Skeeto on the App Store

Don't have Skeeto yet? Look for Skeeto on the App Store on your iPhone, then open this link again.

  • Your timetable comes from ACORN in one sign-in, nothing to type.
  • New friends see busy times only: when you're free, with no course names or rooms.
  • You choose what each person sees, and can stop sharing any time.

Opening this link doesn't add anyone. Skeeto shows you who it is and asks first. Friends are kept to one campus, so you can add each other only if you're both at the same one.

Skeeto is an independent app and is not affiliated with the University of Toronto.
